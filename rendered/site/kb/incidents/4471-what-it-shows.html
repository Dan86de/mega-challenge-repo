How one ticket swallowed three problems

Triage merges on subject line alone (decision 0001). All three problems produce the same subjects: an invite on the wrong day. And the intake tag could not catch it either: the table’s “tagged as” column shows intake guessing parser and hint on both sides, because the tag comes from the reporter’s browser, while the cause depends on the tenant’s locale, form and channel.

Where the counts come from

Nothing in the table above is typed in: ProblemTable computes it at render time from reports-4471.json. The export holds overlapping pages, so reports are deduplicated by id first (uniqueById). Each report then counts once, under the problem it actually describes: the desk’s oneDayMove notes take precedence, then causeOf splits parser from hint. This differs from the incident page’s CauseTable only in that the one-day moves are counted as their own problem instead of footnoted where they were filed.
